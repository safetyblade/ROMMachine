# BRAND-01 / B1 — CARTDECK visual foundation

Status: Sienna design proposal following owner direction, 18 November 2026. Design-only; not APK acceptance or authority to modify runtime. Existing approved C-slot and wordmark are fixed. B2/B3 develop product marks; B4 develops feature and icon assets; B5/B6 deliver components and verified integration.

## Identity and architecture

Think manufactured consumer gaming hardware and a well-organised game collection, not arcade nostalgia or an edgy gaming dashboard. Warm, familiar shapes, deliberate alignment, calm typographic rhythm, tactile materials. Retain the original geometry, lettering and logo lockup at design/cartdeck-v03/brand/. Use the existing C-slot alone for compact marks; use full lockup for launch/About/roomy header. No slant, outline, redrawing, warping, glow or animation that obstructs reading. Monochrome/tint through semantic foreground. Proposed minimum C-slot 24dp, wordmark 112dp, lockup 136dp, clear space half the mark height; at narrow widths prefer mark + accessible CARTDECK name, never shrink illegibly. Publisher by prestwich.dev belongs in About/launch or attribution, not repeated on every screen.

Master brand CARTDECK. Adult destinations in current approved order: Shelf, GEM, SafeSave, CARTMOD, Settings. Settings is a first-class functional destination with Appearance as its lead feature. Junior is an alternate player experience, not an adult dock item; CARTDECK Junior / My Playroom / Grown-Ups. Use fixed canonical spelling, no alternate cases. PivotArc and GripArc are signature control technologies and are shown as true semibold italics in brand contexts/engraving (capital P/G and A); ordinary body references may be italic where supported, accessibility names plain. Their approved hardware art is unchanged. Modern (Settings / Controllers) and Vault (SafeSave) are nested products with their own marks, wordmarks and workspaces, not adult navigation destinations. GEMRating, GemFinder and DeckFit are named capabilities. Legacy internal identifiers may remain. Patch Studio, GEM My Sources, PC Game Setup, My Appearances, profiles, controller settings and Grown-Ups are functional features; use coherent title + icon, not independent logos by default. B4 may propose selected feature marks for owner review. Roxy and project team are not in product copy, images, avatars, recommendations or onboarding; a final-release Easter egg is separate discretionary approval, never a dependency. Existing catalogue/editorial field names may stay internal; don't rename schemas or conflate scores.

## Typography

Preferred candidate Source Sans 3, a restrained humanist UI sans family with genuine italics, 400/500/600/700 upright and 600 italic. Source: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans (SIL OFL 1.1). Existing wordmark remains vector, never replaced by type. Prior v03 specification excludes bundled font binaries: B1 proposes changing that only after owner approval and verified license/package review. No binaries added. Android system sans-serif fallback for unavailable glyphs, platform accessibility scaling retained. If font packaging is not approved, use Android system sans with the same role scale, genuine italic where supported. No network font requirement. Monospace only for hashes, paths and diagnostic IDs, tabular numbers for ratings and status figures when supported. Title case/sentence case, no all-caps paragraph or wide tracked text; hardware badges and product names exceptions. No artificial oblique or fake bold.

Role sizes are proposed sp (size/line height), weight: display 32/38 600; page 24/30 600; section 20/26 600; card 17/22 600; body 16/22 400; secondary 14/20 400; action 15/20 600; label 13/18 500; caption 12/16 400; hardware 12/16 600; signature 20/26 600 italic. Size tokens are baseline, not fixed-height boxes. No essential text below 12sp. TV uses 1.25–1.5x readable roles and focus, based on actual viewport/distance. Increase available height or scroll at 1.3x/2x system font scale; long names wrap or ellipsize with a complete accessible name. Never reduce shelf geometry, actual input targets or gameplay viewport solely to fit a logo or font. Preserve game art and Unicode titles.

## Semantic colours, materials and personalisation

Original approved hardware palette remains #303B49 chassis, #1C2430 secondary, #0D1721 bezel, #4ED8C4 accent, #EDF6F2 ink. B1 proposed neutral UI palette (not an override of current edition persistence): room #151A20, surface #202731, raised #2B3440, outline #526171, ink #F1F3F4, secondary ink #BAC4CE, accent #4ED8C4, on-accent #132421. Owner-approved warm Light appearance: #F4F2ED room, #FFFFFF surface, #E8E5DF raised, #788189 outline, #202630 ink, #4D5965 secondary ink, #146E66 accent, #FFFFFF on-accent; add as a selectable appearance in B6; Original remains default. Success, warning, error, info and focus use semantic roles distinct from brand accents. Meaning is labelled, never colour alone. Minimum text contrast 4.5:1; large text and essential non-text indicators 3:1 against adjacent surfaces. Resolve user selected colours via existing contrast protections; retain safe reset/preview.

Layers: identity/geometry invariant > platform/system family > edition materials > user customisation > semantic safety. Edition/custom choices can affect material colours, pattern, stamp, decals, optional lighting (off by default), plus future approved typography alternatives only for safe display/label roles. Keep body legibility, status meaning, logo geometry and game art independent. Owner-approved appearance-reactive logos: preserve all logo geometry and wording, resolve tint from the active appearance's contrast-safe ink/accent roles (including Light); fall back to readable monochrome where needed. Product accent is optional in Original and Light only; never override a player's chosen appearance. This is a planned presentation change, not existing runtime functionality. Keep existing Original/Pink/Green and special editions unchanged; this palette is a design reference, not migration. Do not expose arbitrary fonts without an approved readable list and accessibility checks. No stealth microtext, default glow, scanlines, pixel fonts, aggressive diagonal cuts, chrome gradients, or decorative texture behind text. Retro special appearances may be chosen, not default.

## UI grammar and states

Use 4dp spacing grid, 8/12/16/24dp primary spacing, 12dp card corners, 8dp small controls, 1dp restrained borders, shallow elevation where separation needed. Preserve existing cartridge geometry and physical bevels; generic screens not skeuomorphic. Product headers use a single mark/title and contextual actions. Owner decision: no promotional taglines or slogans in any product, feature, Junior lockup, introduction or user-facing specimen. Retain only necessary factual task descriptions. Cards present a concrete action, short supporting text and real status. Hide detailed instructions until requested; no decorative dead controls. Keep navigation, filters, add, continue, Back, undo, focus, and accessibility available; protect portrait five-slot shelf and landscape game visibility. Search in Filters (newer owner decision supersedes HOT03 pinned search). 48dp minimum actual touch targets; do not reduce content by stacking brand chrome. On short landscape use compact horizontal action rows and scrollable content. TV uses clear focus, not cursor-dependent affordances.

Icon grammar: 24-unit viewBox, nominal 1.75–2-unit consistent stroke, round joins where appropriate, simple silhouette, optical balance; 24dp standard, 20dp compact, 16dp metadata only, inside >=48dp actionable target. Filled/outlined selection allowed with label/selection semantics, not color alone. Distinct product marks later B2/B3; everyday actions shared. Do not reuse console manufacturer marks or protected game art as product marks. Pressed/focus/disabled/error/loading/empty states must be designed, reduce motion supported, no decorative animation delaying actions or gameplay. Navigation never mounts unavailable feature or manager-only action without real authorization.
