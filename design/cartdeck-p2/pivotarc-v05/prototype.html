CARTDECK PivotArc v0.5

Offline interaction study of the owner's second sketch. Not an Android gamepad or an ergonomics test. One active layout is global across games; the set of required inputs depends on the system.

PivotArc design controls

Control topology and available space

The whole drawn control group moves as one. Start/Select/Menu must occupy a separately validated utility location; that location is not included in the 285 × 300dp core bounds. Video is never drawn or manipulated here.

Adjust Position

Nudge entire group by one step. On compact devices adjustments will clamp near the safe-area edge.

Contact ownership

Pointer-down activates only that button. Moving the same pointer across another circle does not activate it; release clears ownership.

System-specific modules

This prototype cannot establish thumb comfort, actual 48dp Android hit regions, D-pad diagonal accuracy, physical capacitive touches, or DS/analog viability.
