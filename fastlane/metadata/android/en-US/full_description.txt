CARTDECK by prestwich.dev transforms an Android device into a personalised retro-gaming console.

Choose games from a cartridge-inspired library, play with responsive on-screen controls or a gamepad, and keep your in-game progress.

The project is currently under development. Console-specific virtual shells, original boot animations, full-screen presentation and controller features are being implemented and verified in controlled stages.

Open-source component credits and licences are supplied in the accompanying source documentation.

CARTDECK does not provide games or firmware. You must supply game files you are legally permitted to use.
